# Tempo

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
Tempo is a single-page web tool that finds songs at a target BPM and assembles a
Spotify-importable playlist. You set a tempo (or "match a song" to copy its BPM,
artist and genre), narrow by genres and include/exclude artists, preview 30s
snippets, then save the playlist to Spotify. BPM comes from ReccoBeats (Spotify's
own tempo endpoint is dead for new apps); previews come from Spotify's embed page.

## Users
- Runners / cyclists building cadence-matched playlists (e.g. 170 BPM for running).
- DJs and music nerds assembling beat-matched sets.
- Anyone who wants "songs that feel like this one" at a steady tempo.
They are tempo-literate or quickly become so; they care about precision and trust.

## Brand & Tone
Tempo is a **precision instrument**, not a neon music-app. Think studio metronome,
VU-meter glow, analog warmth, confident and exact. Tone: assured, musical,
uncluttered. The product should feel like a well-made physical device.

## Strategic principles
- The BPM is the hero. The tempo number and its musical character lead every screen.
- Honor music literacy: surface Italian tempo markings (Largo, Andante, Allegro,
  Presto) that map to the chosen BPM. This is a signature, meaningful detail.
- Spotify green is a **semantic** color: it appears only on Spotify actions (connect,
  save). Tempo's own identity color is warm amber/gold, so the brand isn't "just a
  Spotify clone."
- Motion should evoke a beating pulse / swinging pendulum, never decorative flourish.

## Anti-references
- Generic dark "music app" with neon green-on-black and purple gradients (the reflex).
- Gradient text, hero-metric SaaS templates, identical icon-card grids.
- Cold cyber/synthwave neon. Tempo is warm and analog, not Tron.
